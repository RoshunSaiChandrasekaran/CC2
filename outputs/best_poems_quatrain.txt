THE FIGURES

Come back to go up in nature,
The little lamb god choose
The figures were the other way
The thoughts that seemed to lose.

The night air and does it not grieve,
Come with the blazing sun,
The night air and corruption leave
Day and the lesson done.

The night a sense of tune,
Oft of the night a roof for breath,

[quatrain #2]  "Love of the Night"
  planned idea: Time is a creditor: we spend our days and still owe.
  moods: melancholic, wordplay, urban

LOVE OF THE NIGHT

What dread hand or nature's changing
I think it will be wires black
Still hopping on what good amid;
The air and jill went snicker snack.

I love thee such a roof for right,
To times in hope love of the night,
Love how I wonder what dread feet,
The roofs of love I seemed to beat.

I grant I love or hear the breath,
Of love as it the hand dare seize,
And on what wings dare frame thy name
I travell'd in the air and trees.

[quatrain #3]  "Silence and Lamb"
  planned idea: Being oneself is hard when several selves compete for the name.
  moods: cynical, dry, cosmic

SILENCE AND LAMB

How public like a little lamb
The little we are called;
Little we passed the ships towers
I like sleeping flowers.

He knew that seals up jack and post,
The silence of the chain
Come to me in eternal lines,
Silence of heaven shines.

The speaking silence of the night;
The wind up hill all bright,
The lamb who are you nobody,
Little bird and men's eyes.
