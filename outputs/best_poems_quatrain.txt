[quatrain #1]  idea: Time is a creditor: we spend our days and still owe.
  moods: detached, wordplay, classical

I mark'd where souls brimfull of gold,
And from time to apollo hold,
Love how long time the main of light,
Long time the tune without the night.

Of proteus rising and gliding,
The rocks the air and ideal grace,
I have been in the night morning,
By the night morning with my face.

  moods: detached, wordplay, classical

The thing with fortune and my friend,
When no more fair sometime declines,
Come to show more fair from sullen
Can no more day by heaven shines.

In the expense of the night sleep
Or call when the night air and weep,
The air so long since then must leave,
In lets out I told it not grieve.

[quatrain #3]  idea: Time is a creditor: we spend our days and still owe.
  moods: detached, wordplay, classical

The deckhand singing with old dame,
Singing his as it came;
In the mason singing his youth,
The morning with my arms.

Up in the night air and spending,
Down and broke his bending
The night sleep in the air and look
The manxome foe he took.
