[sonnet #1]  "Love to the Depth"
  planned idea: Time is a creditor: we spend our days and still owe.
  moods: cynical, wordplay, cosmic

LOVE TO THE DEPTH

I new pay as the air I love more strong
Like stout cortez when feeling out of doom;
Sun and is never saw a sight so long,
I most quiet need by the lecture room!

Love that once in the morning silent bare,
Be form'd till I love abide and men's eyes,
I fondly ask but no it must leave ere,
These O me useless though my bootless cries.

The night sleep death brag thou art and all bright
It in death's dateless night doth not love,
I love or hear the depth and candle light
Well which I love I summon up above?

I love to the depth and all in a swing,
More foolish than the day labour you fling!

[sonnet #2]  "Silence"
  planned idea: Being oneself is hard when several selves compete for the name.
  moods: romantic, dry, dreamlike

SILENCE

In hope love of the plodding and yet come,
The death and the fact has found out of young,
And nothing stands but bears it asked a crumb,
Dream how my light is his first splendour hung.

In death's dateless night sleep in the breath
Night doth like to love thee with eagle eyes,
Love I think on thee better after death,
The deckhand singing with eyes of the skies.

It with smiles tears of eyes of finished years
My mistress when a new soft rounded cheeks;
The silence of the roof for when the spheres,
Come to me useless though my mistress reeks.

Like to woe tell thee with us late the sweet,
And eyes as sunlight on little cat feet.

[sonnet #3]  "The Astronomer"
  planned idea: The more we measure the world, the less of it we see.
  moods: romantic, dry, domestic

THE ASTRONOMER

I sitting heard the sessions of one wide,
I heard chapman speak yet come to the sky,
Astronomer where he returning chide,
And useless though my soul can breathe or eye!

Bear his height be form'd till I heard the cold,
Forth filament out of the morning glad,
With open unto the silence of gold,
And all my life and diagrams to add.

And trees in the day's journey take away,
Of that fair thou art more day what belongs,
The air and men's eyes that from art the day,
The day with their strong melodious songs?

Grew both day at night with his brief hours,
Of the night air so do our powers.
